STEP 1
Prospect Ingestion & Adding Leads
Open the Studio at http://localhost:4000/dashboard or your live Cloudflare URL. Click the ➕ Add Prospect button on the header. Fill in the prospect's full name, email, company, and role (e.g. Pine Script Strategy Developer). Keep ⚡ Run Gemini 3.6 Flash checked to immediately generate their custom outreach draft.
💡
Pro Tip: Search LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and TradingView for creators with keywords like "HMM", "Pine Script v5", "Algo Trader", or "Systematic Futures".
STEP 2
Reviewing & Authorizing Cold Outreach (Approvals)
Navigate to the 🛡️ Approvals tab. Here you will see cards generated by Gemini 3.6 Flash. Read the subject line and the ultra-concise body copy. You have 3 options:
  • ✅ Approve & Authorize: Pushes the lead into the queue for safe Instantly delivery.
  • ✏️ Edit Copy: Allows modifying any line in the email before sending.
  • ❌ Reject: Drops the draft so it is never contacted.
STEP 3
Safe Delivery & Anti-Spam Throttling
Once approved, the system automatically synchronizes with your Instantly.ai account. It enforces a strict limit of 15–25 emails per day per mailbox to preserve 100% inbox placement and domain reputation.
STEP 4
Inbound Reply Classification & Closing Users
When a recipient replies, the webhook logs the response directly to Neon DB. Gemini 3.6 analyzes their sentiment inside the 💬 Replies & AI tab. When an objection or demo request is detected, Gemini drafts the optimal reply to onboard them into Trading OS beta!
